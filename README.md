## ⚡ About Me

<img align="right" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/qgQUggAC3Pfv687qPC/giphy.gif" width="280" style="border-radius:10px; margin-left: 20px;">

I am **José El Asmar**, a Computer Engineer from **UCAB** (Universidad Católica Andrés Bello), passionate about the transformative impact of technology. I have developed strong skills in programming and systems analysis, complemented by critical thinking, teamwork, and leadership.

I am deeply interested in how technology connects with real life to solve challenges. I actively seek to keep learning, collaborate on purposeful projects, and grow alongside forward-thinking people.

### 🛠️ Core Competencies

- 🏗️ **Scalable Architecture:** Proficient in **Clean Architecture** and **Domain-Driven Design (DDD)**, applied effectively to modular Frontends (React) and robust Backends (NestJS).
- 🔐 **Security & Auth:** Implementing secure authentication flows using **OAuth2, JWT, and RBAC** (Role-Based Access Control) following industry best practices to protect data integrity.
- 🎨 **Modern UI/UX:** Building **Responsive and Adaptive** interfaces. I focus on modern design principles for both Web (Material UI) and Mobile (Flutter) to ensure pixel-perfect experiences.
- ⚡ **Performance & State:** Mastering complex state management and optimizing application performance for high-load environments.
- 🔄 **API Design:** Expertise in designing efficient **RESTful APIs** and handling asynchronous communication patterns.
